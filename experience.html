about

Hi, I'm Sagar

Graphics engineer, creator and teacher. The person behind rgbguy.

Graphics engineer. Nine years of building experiences that run at high framerates in the real world.

I started as a graphics and game developer, then moved to XR research at Qualcomm and filed patents. After that came native rendering pipelines, camera SDKs and FaceAR products, scaled to millions of users across Android and Web. Not just shipping features. Architecting the systems underneath them.

Diving deep into graphics has always been the dream. So in 2024 I went back to first principles. I rebuilt concepts from the ground up and implemented rendering algorithms on multiple graphics APIs and platforms. Then I turned the learnings into videos that 300K+ learners watch.

Right now I'm building a 3D engine from scratch in C/C++. Because the best way to understand a system is to build it yourself.

The rare kind of engineer who reads papers, ships production code, and teaches it to a room full of people. Sometimes all in the same week.
